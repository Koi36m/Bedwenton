## Visual change

Read `../M8-K1.1-VISUAL-POLISH.md` for the complete additive visual specification.
The waist is now a constant-radius rear crescent, not a 360-degree ring. The existing M3 circular-family `Spiral.arc`
API samples a planar, zero-rise arc; no spiral visual behavior is introduced. Local -Z means behind the body.
Simple/Balanced/Heavy spans are 120/160/200 degrees. The existing +Y body-yaw anchor is unchanged.

| Tier | Waist END_ROD | Cyan halo | Total/reservation | Previous total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Simple |8|0|8|12|
| Balanced |12|6|18|27|
| Heavy |16|8|24|36|

All tiers reduce total points by one third. Waist radius 0.9 and feet +1.0 remain. The waist's 60-tick full revolution
becomes a smooth -8 to +8 degree sway and back, using existing M2 pingPong(30), M3 SMOOTHSTEP and M5 Motion.spin.
A full revolution would bring the entire crescent into the front, defeating the requested rear bias. The sway starts
at -8 degrees and stays rear-centered at all phases. Simple/Balanced points remain entirely behind the body; Heavy
keeps at least 13/16 points behind and leaves a 144-degree front wedge clear, including sway extremes.

The halo remains cyan `0x5CE1FF`, radius 0.45, feet +2.15, with its original opposite 80-tick spin. Counts decrease
9/12 to 6/8 and DUST size 0.7 to 0.55. Height is unchanged; reduced visual crowding is the goal, not a measured
nameplate-clearance guarantee. Style keys, native payload families and two layers are retained. Geometry is constructed
once per session. No new task, history/buffer, effect family, spark node, trail, dependency, gameplay operation,
NMS/reflection, global broadcast or chunk loading was added. Simple remains the configured default.
