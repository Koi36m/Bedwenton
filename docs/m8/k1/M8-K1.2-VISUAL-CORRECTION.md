## Exact placement and motion

The complete white outline has radius **0.78 blocks**, centered **1.25 blocks above feet** and **0.65 blocks backward**.
It lies in the player-local **XY plane** at `z=-0.65`, so its point formula is
`(0.78*cos(a), 1.25+0.78*sin(a), -0.65)`. The existing body-yaw anchor maps local -Z behind the player every tick.
Pitch never tilts it. The outline is stable; it does not sway, revolve around the waist, swing in front or turn edge-on.
M3's existing XZ circle is sampled by M4 with EXCLUDE_END, then placed vertically by an M1 +90-degree X rotation
inside M5 fixed motion. No engine change or new curve type is needed.

Two white END_ROD cubic sweeps form an asymmetric flowing S. Relative to the disc center their XY control points are:

| Stroke | P0 | P1 | P2 | P3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | (-0.62,-0.24) | (-0.18,-0.38) | (-0.06,-0.16) | (-0.08,0.02) |
| B | (-0.08,0.02) | (-0.12,0.38) | (0.28,0.32) | (0.56,0.30) |

All control-point Z values are zero. Tangents align at the shared junction; stroke B omits that already emitted point.
Every control point has radius below 0.665, so the Bézier convex hull is strictly inside the outer radius 0.78.
The two unequal sweeps avoid 180-degree symmetry. They rotate continuously, **one full revolution every 80 ticks**,
about **local +Z**, the disc normal. M2 loop(80), M3 LINEAR and M5 spin drive rotation relative to the original start tick.
Translation to `(0,1.25,-0.65)` is the parent motion, so rotation never moves the center or changes the plane.
The formation is outline plus curved lines, not a filled disc. Geometry is sampled once per enabled session and reused.
