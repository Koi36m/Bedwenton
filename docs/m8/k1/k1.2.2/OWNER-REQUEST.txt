## Why this pass is needed

The K1.2.1 back-mounted sigil now has the correct size and placement, but in Minecraft the white END_ROD particles visually accumulate into a dense glowing mass.

This makes the rotating inner design hard to read.

The owner wants to keep the END_ROD aesthetic, but reduce visual crowding.

## Objective

Implement a **clarity pass only**:

- keep the white END_ROD sigil
- keep the cyan halo
- keep the same geometry and placement
- keep the same 80-tick inner rotation
- reduce white particle crowding by emitting the white sigil less frequently

## Required visual change

### White sigil emission cadence

Do not attempt to change vanilla END_ROD lifetime directly.

Instead, reduce the emission cadence of the **white back-mounted sigil**.

Required behavior:
- the white outer ring and white inner rotating strokes should emit less often than every tick
- target: emit the white sigil **once every 4 ticks**
- preserve the same geometric positions sampled from the current design
- preserve the same rotation phase progression over time
- preserve the same vertical outline and rotating internal design
- preserve the same tier geometry and counts:
  - Simple: 20
  - Balanced: 34
  - Heavy: 44

Interpretation:
- the sigil’s underlying motion/rotation still advances continuously according to the current deterministic timing model
- only the frequency of emitted white particles is reduced

### Cyan halo

Keep the cyan halo behavior unchanged unless a very small technical adjustment is absolutely necessary.

The owner specifically chose to reduce END_ROD clutter rather than replace the effect with another particle type.
